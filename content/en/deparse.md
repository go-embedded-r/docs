`syntax.Deparse` is in
[v0.2.0](https://github.com/go-embedded-r/r/releases/tag/v0.2.0) and completes
milestone M1 (parser and deparse).

## What it is written from

Deparse is written from the documented behaviour of `deparse()`: precedence and
parenthesisation, the 60-byte line cutoff with its break points and
indentation, `if`/`else` layout inside braces, `structure`-free constants,
15-significant-digit numbers, and string escapes with R's own table of
printable characters. That table is generated by the oracle into
`syntax/print_gen.go`, and the list of primitives that deparse as function calls
into `syntax/prims_gen.go`. No R source is copied.

## Parity with R 4.6.2

Judged by the oracle: the same input is parsed, then deparsed by R and by Go,
for `deparse()` and `print()`.

| Corpus | Inputs | Deparse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R `src/library`: every `.R` file of the base packages (7.8 MB) | 844 (842 parse, 2 rejected) | **842 / 842** |
| R `tests/`: 95 files (1 is not UTF-8 and is skipped) | 94 (90 parse, 4 rejected) | **90 / 90** |
| our snippets | 42 | **42 / 42** |
| our deparse cases (parentheses, precedence, line breaking, indentation, constants, names) | 65 (2 rejected) | **63 / 63** |
| differential fuzz: 610 000 random edits of lines of R's sources | 610 000 | **all** |
| decimal literals, random (up to 20 digits, exponents -330..330) | 100 000 | 99 995 identical, 5 not |

The complete table, with the tree, token and error columns, is on
[The parser]({{< relref "parser" >}}).

## Known divergences

Both are platform effects, not semantics.

- **`exp10`.** Numeric constants with a decimal exponent beyond about 200 in
  magnitude: R takes a power of ten from the C library's `exp10`, which on
  macOS is not always the correctly rounded value. 5 of 100 000 random
  literals of that kind (`1e229`, `1e303`, `1e-244`) deparse with one more digit
  in Go (`1.00000000000000e+229`). No such constant occurs in R's sources or
  tests.
- **x86-64 `long double`.** On x86-64 Linux, where R parses and formats
  numbers in 80-bit `long double` with its own digits routine, 6 of the 90
  files of R's `tests/` deparse with a different last digit in some constant
  (`1e-270` against `9.99999999999999e-271`, `-5152.07935578853` against
  `...52`). The CI lane on Linux reports that verdict and gates the other
  three columns and all of `src/library`.
